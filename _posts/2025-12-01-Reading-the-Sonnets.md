Almost 5 months ago, I set out on a bold (foolish?) project: to record all 154 of Shakespeare's Sonnets, publishing them one at a time, for 154 days. The original goal was to get comfortable reading into a microphone, as I was interested in reading more of my stories and essays for publication. Also I love Shakespear. Very quickly I realized that I was working toward the bigger goal of recording my novel, *Echo Future Truth* (previously: *Echoes of a Future Truth*). The sonnet project is done, and you can hear every Sonnet by visiting (Reading the Sonnets)[https://readingthesonnets.substack.com], where you can also read more about the project and my foolishness. 

Now, on to the really big (also foolish?) project .... Stay tuned.
